App Support

How can we help?

Manifestra is a personal affirmation, visualization, vision board, and journaling app for iOS. If something is not working as expected, or you have a question about your account, subscription, or data, we are here to help.

Contact support

Email us and we will get back to you as soon as we can, typically within a few business days.

Email: supportappsusa@gmail.com

For faster help, include the device you use, your iOS version, the app version if you can find it, and a short description of what happened.

Subscriptions

Manifestra Pro subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple through your Apple ID.

  • To view, change, or cancel a subscription, open the App Store, tap your profile picture, then tap Subscriptions.
  • Deleting the app does not cancel an active subscription.
  • Current prices and billing periods are shown in the app before purchase.

Restore purchases

If Manifestra Pro does not appear active after reinstalling the app or moving to a new device, use Restore Purchases from the paywall or Settings.

  • Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
  • If restore still does not work, contact us with any Apple receipt details you can share.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your Manifestra account from inside the app.

  • Open Settings in Manifestra and choose Delete Account.
  • This removes your sign-in account, cloud-synced content stored under that account, and local app data on the device.
  • Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Privacy and data

Manifestra is designed to be private by default. Most content you create stays on your device.

  • Vision board photos are stored locally and are not uploaded to Manifestra cloud storage.
  • Optional sign-in and cloud sync use Firebase for account and sync features.
  • See the Privacy Policy for complete details.

Quick troubleshooting

  • Reminders are not appearing: Check iOS Settings > Notifications > Manifestra and confirm notifications are allowed.
  • Photos cannot be added: Check iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos and allow Manifestra to access selected photos or your photo library.
  • Cloud content is missing: Confirm you are signed in with the same Apple or Google account used previously.
  • The app feels stuck: Force quit Manifestra, reopen it, and check that your internet connection is working if you use cloud sync or subscriptions.

Wellbeing note

Manifestra offers reflective, motivational content for personal growth. It is not a medical or mental-health service and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are experiencing an emergency or crisis, contact local emergency services or a qualified crisis line immediately.
