Manifestra Legal & Privacy

Manifestra is a personal affirmation, vision board, and journaling app for iOS. These documents explain what the app does with your information and the terms under which it is provided.

At a glance

  • Most of what you create stays on your device.
  • Optional cloud sync uses Google Firebase and is scoped to your user ID.
  • No advertising networks and no third-party marketing analytics.
  • Sign in with Apple and Google are optional; you can also use the app as a guest.
