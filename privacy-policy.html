Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 21, 2026

Manifestra is a personal affirmation and goal-setting application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your rights with respect to that information.

By using Manifestra, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please stop using the app and uninstall it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Manifestra, you may provide the following information directly within the app:

  • Your first name or display name
  • Personal goals and life improvement areas selected during onboarding
  • Journal entries, daily intentions, and reflections
  • Affirmations you create or save
  • Vision board content, including images selected from your photo library
  • Reminder time preferences

If cloud features are enabled in your version of the app, we may also process account information through Google Firebase Authentication, including a unique user identifier, and — if you choose to sign in with Google or Apple — your email address and display name where provided.

If you purchase a Manifestra Pro subscription, your purchase is processed by Apple, and we use RevenueCat to validate and manage your subscription status. This involves a subscription identifier and purchase/entitlement information. We never receive or store your full payment card details; those are handled by Apple.

Manifestra does not use third-party advertising networks or marketing analytics services, and does not use OneSignal or Mixpanel. Internal analytics events are written only to the developer debug console during development and are not transmitted off your device.

2. How Your Information Is Stored

Most of what you create — including journal entries, reflections, and vision board photos — is stored on your device using local storage and the device file system.

If cloud features are enabled, a limited amount of app content and metadata (your profile and personalization data, vision board metadata excluding the photo files, daily statistics and streaks, favorites, and self-love challenge progress) may be stored under your user ID in Google Cloud Firestore so it can be backed up and synced. Journal entries and vision board photo files are not uploaded to our cloud in the current version.

Cloud data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google Firebase and may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States.

Important: If you delete or uninstall the Manifestra app, locally stored data may be permanently deleted. We recommend noting any content you wish to keep before uninstalling.

3. Photo Library Access

If you add images to your Vision Board, Manifestra requests access to your photo library. This access is used solely to let you select images to display within the app. Selected images are stored on your device and are not uploaded to our cloud.

You can revoke photo library access at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security > Photos.

4. Notification Access

Manifestra may request permission to send local notifications to deliver your daily reminder. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve our servers. No notification content is transmitted or logged.

You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings under Notifications > Manifestra.

5. Children's Privacy

Manifestra is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the app, please contact us at supportappsusa@gmail.com.

6. Data Security

On-device data is protected by your device's own security, including your passcode, biometrics, and iOS app sandboxing. We recommend using a device passcode and keeping your iOS software up to date.

Cloud data, where enabled, is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google Firebase and is scoped to your user ID. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

Manifestra uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) for optional account and cloud sync
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple for optional sign-in
  • Apple In-App Purchase, which processes Manifestra Pro subscription payments
  • RevenueCat, which validates and manages subscription and entitlement status
  • Google Fonts, which may fetch and cache font files on your device at runtime
  • The Apple App Store for distribution

We do not use OneSignal, Mixpanel, third-party advertising networks, or marketing analytics services.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability and withdrawal of consent.

  • Access: Your on-device data is accessible to you directly through the app at all times.
  • Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and its data using "Delete Account" in the app (Settings), which removes your Firebase account and the content stored under it, and clears local data on your device. You may also contact us at the email below.
  • Portability: You can note or copy content from the app at any time.

To exercise these rights, or to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, contact us at the email in the Contact section.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this document. If the changes are significant, we will provide a notice within the app. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
